GARMS is a thrift streetwear e-commerce website focused on creating a simple but expressive online shopping experience.

The project combines product browsing, categories, product details, sizes, quantities, wishlist functionality, cart functionality, checkout, and a custom Build Your Fit feature.

The interface was designed around a bold streetwear identity while keeping the experience clear and easy to navigate.
